A shared language that we (humans), agents, code and UI all speak.

## Organization aka Org
- This is the the Tenant in a multi-tenant system.
- It usually represents 1 organization/NGO.

## Organization Unit aka Org Unit

## Root Organization Unit
- This is Organization Unit that is directly connected to the Organization (1:1). It has no parent Organization Unit.

## Members
- A user becomes a member when they have an accepted membership in an Organization Unit.
- By default a user becomes a member of an organization when they join one of it's Organization Units.

## Role
- The available Roles are attached and managed on the Organization.
- A user gets assigned a role when they join an Organization Unit and become a member.
- Being assigned a Role means you get permissions in that Organization Unit and any of its child Organisation Units.

## Volunteer
- A member who has only the View Org (backoffice) permission in the Organization Unit.

## Adminstrator aka Admin
- A member who has more than the View Org permission in the Organization Unit.

## QR iD
- A personal and unique QR code for each user.
- Used to check that person into a Shift.

## Volunteering side
- Primarily for volunteers - they can find and join shifts, their QR iD, requuirement profiles and time worked.
- It's everything a volunteer needs.

## Backoffice side
- Adminstritive side
- Shifts are created, planned and managed.
- Volunteers and their information (timesheets, requirement profiles) are managed.

Note: Permissions are more like Backoffice permissions. If they have any backoffice permissions, then they can access the backoffice
